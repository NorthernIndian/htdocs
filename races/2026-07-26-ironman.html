I started the run after Dirk came in from the bike. It was getting close to noon by then, so it got hot as fuck pretty quickly.

I had so much fun on this run, mostly because I was passing people pretty much the entire race. I was also very grateful for all the locals who were out spraying us down with their hoses. Those little cooldowns felt amazing in the heat.

Since I wasn't chasing a PB on the hilly course, one of my goals was to experiment with running at a higher heart rate than I normally would for a half marathon. I settled in around 160 bpm and basically held it there to see what would happen.

It turns out I can handle it pretty damn well. I averaged 158 bpm for the race and actually increased my effort as it went on. I felt strong almost the entire way and didn't really start hurting until the final 1.5 km.

I finished the 21.35 km in 1:46:03. The time itself wasn't anything special for me, but that wasn't really the point of this race. The biggest takeaway was learning that I'm capable of sustaining a much higher heart rate for a long period of time than I had previously given myself credit for.
